Exhibit III

Modern Naval Operations

Modern naval intelligence combines cybersecurity, satellite systems, surveillance technologies, digital communication networks, and real-time mission coordination to support military operations around the world.

Unlike earlier eras that relied mainly on observation and physical communication, modern naval operations depend heavily on data, technology, artificial intelligence, and global monitoring systems that can respond to threats almost instantly.

Modern Intelligence Systems

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity systems protect military networks, communication systems, databases, and classified information from cyber attacks and digital threats.

As warfare becomes more connected to technology, cybersecurity plays a major role in protecting intelligence operations and maintaining operational security.

Satellite Surveillance

Satellite systems support navigation, intelligence gathering, communication, and global monitoring across large geographic regions.

These technologies provide real-time information that helps military leaders track movement, identify threats, and coordinate missions across the world.

Mission Planning

Intelligence teams analyze large amounts of data to support strategic planning, operational coordination, and military decision-making.

Modern mission planning combines communication systems, surveillance data, digital mapping, and intelligence analysis to improve operational success.

Museum Reflection

Modern naval intelligence demonstrates how warfare and security operations now depend on information technology, communication networks, and digital systems.

The transition from visual observation to advanced surveillance and cybersecurity reflects how intelligence operations continue to evolve alongside global technology.
