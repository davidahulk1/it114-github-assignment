The Legacy of Naval Intelligence

Welcome to the Museum

This museum explores the evolution of naval intelligence from early sea navigation to modern warfare systems. It highlights communication, radar, cryptography, surveillance, cybersecurity, and strategic military operations.

Exhibit Sections

1. Early Naval Intelligence

Learn how early naval forces used maps, coded signals, flags, and navigation systems to gain strategic advantages at sea.

2. Technology and Warfare

Discover how radar, sonar, cryptography, and intelligence systems transformed naval warfare and national security.

3. Modern Naval Operations

Explore cybersecurity, satellite surveillance, intelligence gathering, and mission planning used in today’s naval operations.
