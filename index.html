Museum Overview

A History of Intelligence at Sea

Naval intelligence has played a major role in warfare, communication, navigation, surveillance, and strategic planning throughout history. From the earliest naval expeditions to modern digital defense systems, intelligence operations helped military forces understand threats, coordinate missions, and protect national security.

This digital museum presents a guided experience through the evolution of naval intelligence. Each exhibit focuses on a different period of development, showing how information, technology, and communication shaped naval operations across time.

Timeline of Naval Intelligence

1700s–1800s

Navigation systems, signal flags, and strategic observation.

1900s–1940s

Radar, sonar, cryptography, and wartime communication systems.

1950s–2000s

Satellite surveillance and global intelligence networks.

Modern Era

Cybersecurity, digital warfare, and real-time intelligence systems.

Design Framework

Design & Narrative Strategy

Swiss Design

Swiss Design principles were used to create clean layouts, visual hierarchy, strong spacing, and a structured museum experience.

Hero Archetype

The Hero archetype shaped the tone of the museum through themes of courage, leadership, service, discipline, and national defense.

Authority Principle

Educational framing, curator reflections, and organized exhibit structure were used to create credibility and visitor trust.
