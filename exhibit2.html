Exhibit II

Technology and Warfare

Radar, sonar, cryptography, and surveillance systems transformed naval warfare and intelligence operations during the modern era.

Technological Evolution

The Rise of Advanced Intelligence Systems

As naval warfare evolved, intelligence systems became more advanced and technology-driven. Radar and sonar allowed ships to detect threats from long distances, while cryptography protected military communication from interception.

These innovations helped naval forces improve mission coordination, submarine detection, strategic planning, and global security operations.

Major Technological Developments

Radar Systems

Radar technology improved threat detection by allowing ships to identify enemy aircraft and vessels during combat operations.

Sonar Technology

Sonar systems became essential for submarine tracking, underwater navigation, and maritime defense strategies.

Cryptography

Encrypted communication protected classified military information and prevented enemy interception during wartime operations.

Museum Reflection

This exhibit highlights how technological innovation reshaped naval intelligence. The development of radar, sonar, and secure communication systems greatly expanded the Navy’s ability to monitor threats and protect national security.
