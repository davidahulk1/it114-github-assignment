Exhibit II

Technology and Warfare

Naval warfare changed dramatically with the introduction of radar, sonar, cryptography, and electronic intelligence systems. These technologies improved communication, enemy detection, mission coordination, and national security operations.

Technological Breakthroughs

Radar Systems

Radar systems allowed naval forces to detect ships and aircraft across long distances during wartime operations.

Cryptography

Encrypted communication protected classified military information and reduced enemy interception during conflict.

Sonar Technology

Sonar systems became critical for submarine detection, underwater navigation, and maritime defense.

Museum Reflection

This exhibit highlights how innovation transformed naval intelligence. Technology expanded the Navy’s ability to gather information, coordinate missions, and respond to global threats.
